INVENTORY POLICY - SmartSupply AI

About this inventory policy: it was written by the SmartSupply team. The rules below are the rules the app uses for every product. The numbers for each product are calculated from the dataset by forecast.py.

Service level: the inventory policy aims for a 95% service level, which means having enough stock in 95 out of 100 order cycles. This gives a safety factor (z) of 1.65.

Safety stock: safety stock = 1.65 x forecast error x square root of the supplier lead time in days. The forecast error is the standard deviation of the daily forecast errors found when the forecast was tested on the last 3 months.

Lead time: the lead time is the supplier's average number of days between placing an order and receiving the products. It comes from the supplier data.

Reorder point: reorder point = average daily forecast x lead time + safety stock.

When to reorder: compare the reorder point with the stock position. The stock position is the stock on hand plus the units already on order. If the stock position is at or below the reorder point, the status is Reorder Now. If it will reach the reorder point within 7 days, the status is Low. Otherwise the status is OK.

How much to order: an order should cover the lead time plus 30 more days of forecast demand, plus the safety stock, minus the stock position. The quantity must be between the supplier's minimum and maximum order quantity.

Who places the order: the app only suggests orders. A person checks the suggestion and places the purchase order with the supplier. Rejected units found during inspection are not added to stock.
